# Guided learning path — 56 lessons

Eight stages. Read, practise and use the checkpoint. Marks record practice, not mastery. Existing lesson IDs and practice marks remain valid. Basic movement and attack inputs are assumed. Supports and junglers may use player-view lane replays; you can skip or revisit any lesson.

## 1. Lane foundations

Read your resources, levels, reach and map before committing.

1. Last-hitting
2. Spacing
3. Experience
4. Resource management
5. Skillshots
6. Dodging
7. Kiting
8. Map awareness

Checkpoint: Review three lane decisions. Identify farm or experience gained or conceded, your available resources, a range threat and map information that changed your action.

## 2. Combat tools

Choose a cast or commitment from observed information.

9. Cooldown tracking
10. Crowd control
11. Summoner spells
12. Combos
13. Trading
14. Poking
15. All-ins
16. Zoning
17. Baiting

Checkpoint: Review a missed spell, a short trade and a committed fight. Name the tool, opening and stop condition. Repeat the drill for the choice you cannot explain.

## 3. Waves and purchases

Choose a wave plan, leave safely and turn income into useful strength.

18. Wave management
19. Fast pushing
20. Slow pushing
21. Wave crashing
22. Wave bouncing
23. Freezing
24. Recalling
25. Itemization
26. Power spikes

Checkpoint: Review three wave decisions and one purchase. Name the wave plan, actual result, departure cost and what the bought item or new level enables.

## 4. Map information

Use observed information to decide whether movement is safe.

27. Warding
28. Vision denial
29. Jungle tracking
30. Lane priority
31. Weak side

Checkpoint: Before three map moves, name a known enemy position, an unknown approach, reachable allied help and a retreat point. Use player view in replays.

## 5. Jungle decisions

Connect jungle routes to lane readiness and safe access.

32. Jungle pathing
33. Ganking
34. Counterganking
35. Invading
36. Counterjungling

Checkpoint: Review a route, a lane approach and an enemy-jungle entry. Name the purpose, evidence, alternative task and exit. Non-junglers can use a teammate replay.

## 6. Connected pressure

38. Resets
39. Roaming
40. Lane assignments
41. Rotations
42. Split pushing
43. Cross-map trading

Checkpoint: Review one reset, rotation and side-lane action. Identify the useful window, the duty left behind and whether teammates could act at the same time.

## 7. Team fights

44. Positioning
45. Target selection
46. Body blocking
47. Peeling
48. Engaging
49. Disengaging
50. Flanking
51. Collapsing

Checkpoint: Review three fights. Name your job, reachable targets, the main threat and the exit. Explain one moment when changing your target or job would help.

## 8. Closing games

Turn temporary advantages into safe progress toward the base.

52. Win conditions
53. Picks
54. Objective control
55. Sieging
56. Diving

Checkpoint: State your current win condition, then review two attempts to make progress. Name the setup, achievable gain and cancellation condition; include an option when behind.
