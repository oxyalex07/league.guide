# Mid lane cheat sheet

- Slug: mid
- Role: Mid
- Summary: Win wave priority in the shortest lane, then use it to roam, help objectives and control the centre of the map.
- Where you play: The middle lane, with quick access to both sides of the river.
- You win by: Wave priority, well-timed roams and teamfight damage.
- Starter champions: annie, lux, malzahar, ahri, galio, veigar
- Key lessons: MI01, MI14, MI09, MI18, MA04, MA14, MA13, MA39, MI12

## Short answer

Mid is the shortest lane and sits in the centre of the map. That makes it the lane with the most influence: a mid laner who pushes first can move to either river, help a gank, cover their jungler or arrive early at an objective. It also makes mid dangerous, because enemies can reach you from both sides. Mid champions are usually mages or assassins who rely on abilities, so dodging, landing skillshots and tracking cooldowns matter as much as farm. The best mid laners turn small lane advantages into map advantages.

## Your jobs

1. **Win wave priority.** Push your wave first so you can move before your opponent.
2. **Use priority well.** Roam, help your jungler in the river or ward when your wave is safe.
3. **Track the enemy mid and jungler.** Ping the moment your opponent leaves lane.
4. **Deal damage in fights.** Mages control zones from range; assassins remove key targets.
5. **Hold the centre later.** In the mid game, the mid wave is the one that most often needs to be cleared.

## Laning phase

- **Last-hit with spacing.** Stay out of range of your opponent's best poke while you farm.
- **Dodge with small movements.** Short sidesteps between attacks avoid more skillshots than long walks.
- **Use poke when they last-hit.** Enemies must step forward to farm; that is when your skillshots land most often.
- **Push before you move.** Clear your wave before leaving so you lose little and your opponent must stay to defend.
- **Ward both sides.** Mid can be ganked from either river. A ward on each side, or on the side the enemy jungler is likely to be, keeps you safe.
- **Fight for the first recall.** Recall after crashing a wave, so you return with an item while your opponent loses farm.

## Mid game

- **Roam with a purpose.** A roam works best when your wave is pushed, the target lane is pushed up and you can arrive unseen. If it fails, return quickly.
- **Follow your jungler.** When your jungler moves to an objective or invade, be ready to join first.
- **Clear mid before objectives.** A wave in mid lets you arrive at dragon or Baron early without giving up a turret.
- **Do not face-check.** As the mid game opens, enemies can hide in the jungle near mid. Let wards or tanks check brushes first.
- **Take side waves carefully.** Farming a side lane is valuable, but know where the enemies are before you walk far from your team.

## Teamfights

- **Mages stay at range.** Hit whoever you can reach safely, and keep your crowd control for divers.
- **Assassins wait.** Let the enemy use key crowd control and spells, then flank to a vulnerable target.
- **Keep track of your key ability.** Many mid champions rely on one big ability. Do not spend it on a target that is about to die anyway.
- **Do not die first.** Your damage matters only while you are alive. Stand behind your front line and use your full range.

## What to track

- When your opponent leaves lane: ping it and warn the lane they are moving to.
- Both junglers: where they were last seen and which side of the map they are on.
- Your opponent's key ability and summoner spells.
- Your lane priority: who can move first right now.

## Common mistakes

- **Mistake:** Roaming with your wave pushing towards you. **Fix:** Clear the wave first so you do not lose farm and a turret.
- **Mistake:** Wasting abilities on minions while the enemy waits to trade. **Fix:** Keep your main ability for trades or safe last-hits.
- **Mistake:** Staying in lane while your jungler fights in the river. **Fix:** Watch the minimap and move first when your wave is pushed.
- **Mistake:** Walking into an unseen jungle area to farm. **Fix:** Farm where you have vision or allies nearby.

## Going deeper

**Priority is a currency.** Every time you push first, you earn a short window to move. Decide how to spend it: a roam, a ward, a river fight or simply a safe recall.

**Know when to stay.** Not every window is a roam. If the other lanes are pushed into their own turrets, a roam often fails. Use the time to farm, ward or pressure the enemy turret instead.

**Pair with your jungler.** Mid and jungle together decide most early river fights. If you both know the plan, you will win more scuttle crab contests and objective fights.

## Practice plan

1. In your next three games, ping every time the enemy mid laner leaves lane.
2. Before each roam, say out loud: wave pushed, target lane set up, path unseen.
3. After each game, count how many times you moved first to a river fight or objective.
