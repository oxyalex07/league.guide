# Jungle cheat sheet

- Slug: jungle
- Role: Jungle
- Summary: Plan your clears, gank lanes that are set up to win, and control objectives with your team.
- Where you play: Both jungles and the river, moving between all three lanes.
- You win by: Efficient clears, well-timed ganks and objective control.
- Starter champions: warwick, amumu, vi, jarvaniv, nunu, sejuani
- Key lessons: MA29, MA27, MA28, MA06, MA22, MA10, MA41, MA30, MA42

## Short answer

The jungler is the only player without a lane. You earn gold and experience from jungle camps, and you choose where to apply pressure: which lane to gank, which objective to take and when to invade. That freedom makes jungle the role with the most decisions per minute. Strong junglers are not the ones who gank the most. They plan each clear so they arrive at the right lane at the right time, take objectives their team can contest, and always have a rough idea of where the enemy jungler is.

## Your jobs

1. **Clear camps efficiently.** Your farm comes from camps. Wasted time walking or waiting puts you behind.
2. **Gank lanes that are set up.** Look for an enemy pushed up, without Flash or low on health, and an ally who can follow up.
3. **Track the enemy jungler.** Predict where they are so you can counter-gank, take the opposite side or warn your lanes.
4. **Control objectives.** Dragons on the bottom side, and Void Grubs, Rift Herald and Baron on the top side. Use Smite to secure them.
5. **Create tempo.** Always have a next step: a camp, a gank, an objective or a recall.

## Early game

- **Take Smite and a jungle companion item.** Smite is required to clear camps quickly and secure objectives. Your companion grows as you take camps.
- **Plan your first clear before the game.** Choose where to start and where to finish. Finish near the lane you most want to gank or the objective you want to take.
- **Start away from danger.** If the enemy jungler is likely to invade one side, start on the other side or ask your team to help.
- **Use the scuttle crab.** Scuttle crabs in the river give vision and gold. Contest them when your nearby lanes have priority.
- **Gank with a plan.** Approach from an angle the enemy cannot see, wait for the enemy to step forward, and use crowd control before damage.
- **Count camps.** If you see the enemy jungler, think about which camps they have taken and where they will go next.

## Mid game

- **Play around the next objective.** Start moving towards the next dragon, Void Grubs, Herald or Baron before it spawns, with your lanes pushed and wards placed.
- **Clear on the side you are not playing.** If your team will fight for dragon, farm top-side camps first, then arrive at bot side on time.
- **Invade when your lanes are winning.** Taking enemy camps is safe when your nearby laners can reach the fight first.
- **Do not over-farm or over-gank.** Farming every camp while objectives are taken loses games; ganking every lane while falling behind in levels does too.
- **Ward and sweep the enemy jungle.** Vision in their jungle tells you their path and keeps your team safe from picks.

## Teamfights and objectives

- **Smite at the right moment.** Learn roughly how much damage Smite deals at your level, and use it when the objective is low enough, not early.
- **Watch the enemy jungler, not just the objective.** If they are close and have Smite, fight them or block them before trying to secure.
- **Your champion decides your job.** Tanks engage or peel; assassins and fighters flank and finish targets.
- **Count enemies before starting.** If an enemy is missing, they may be about to arrive or taking something elsewhere.

## What to track

- The enemy jungler's last known position, their likely next camps and their buff timers.
- Objective spawn timers and which team has priority near them.
- Each lane's wave state: who is pushed, who is low and who has Flash.
- Your next three steps: what you do now, next and after that.

## Common mistakes

- **Mistake:** Ganking a lane where the enemy is safe under their turret. **Fix:** Gank lanes where the enemy is pushed up or has no escape.
- **Mistake:** Arriving at an objective just as it spawns, without vision or pushed lanes. **Fix:** Plan the setup a minute or more before the spawn.
- **Mistake:** Farming camps while a fight starts nearby. **Fix:** Watch the minimap; join fights you can reach in time.
- **Mistake:** Using Smite too early in a contested objective. **Fix:** Wait until the objective is in Smite range and the enemy jungler is accounted for.

## Going deeper

**Think in tempo, not kills.** A gank that takes two minutes and forces only a Flash may be worse than finishing a clear and taking a dragon. Value what each action gives your team.

**Mirror the enemy jungler.** When you know where they are, do the opposite: take objectives on the other side, or move to counter-gank if your lane can fight.

**Talk to your lanes.** Ping where you are going and when. A laner who knows you are coming can push, slow down or hold their abilities for your gank.

## Practice plan

1. Before your next three games, write your first clear and where it should finish.
2. When you first see the enemy jungler, ping it and guess their next camp.
3. After each game, review one objective: when did you start moving, and were your lanes pushed?
