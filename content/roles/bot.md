# Bot lane cheat sheet

- Slug: bot
- Role: Bot
- Summary: Farm and scale safely with your support, then deal steady damage from the back of every fight.
- Where you play: The bottom lane with your support, close to the dragon pit.
- You win by: Farm, item spikes and staying alive in fights.
- Starter champions: ashe, missfortune, caitlyn, jinx, sivir, ezreal
- Key lessons: MI01, MI07, MI10, MI12, MI22, MA20, MA21, MA10, MI23

## Short answer

The bot laner, often called the marksman or ADC, is usually the team's main source of steady damage. You start weaker than most roles but become very strong with items, so farm is your top priority. You share the lane with a support, which makes bot a two-versus-two lane where both players' cooldowns and positions matter. Later, your job in fights is simple to say and hard to do: stay alive and keep attacking whatever you can reach safely. Bot laners who climb are excellent at farming, spacing and choosing their targets.

## Your jobs

1. **Farm as much as you safely can.** Your gold buys the items that make you strong.
2. **Play with your support.** Trade when they can trade, back off when they cannot.
3. **Help secure dragons.** Bot lane priority makes dragons much easier for your team.
4. **Hit your item spikes.** Recall when you have gold for an important item and the wave allows it.
5. **Deal damage without dying.** In fights, attack the closest safe target and keep moving.

## Laning phase

- **Last-hit first.** Every missed minion delays your next item. Practise last-hitting under your turret too, where minions take turret damage.
- **Space against the enemy support.** Many supports have crowd control that starts a kill. Stay out of its range or keep a minion between you.
- **Trade when your support is ready.** A support with crowd control or a shield available makes trades much safer.
- **Race to level 2.** In a two-versus-two lane, the first duo to level 2 can often force a strong trade. Push the first wave quickly with your support.
- **Watch for ganks.** Bot lane is a common gank target. If your lane is pushed and you have no vision, play safer.
- **Recall at the right time.** Crash the wave and recall when you can buy something meaningful.

## Mid game

- **Move with your team.** After the first turrets fall, bot laners often move to mid or follow the team. A lone marksman in a side lane is an easy target.
- **Farm the safest wave.** Take waves closest to your team and vision. Let others hold the far side lanes.
- **Arrive early to objectives.** Dragons and Baron are easier with your damage. Push your wave, then go.
- **Respect your spikes.** You are often much stronger after your second or third main item. Before then, avoid long fights you cannot win.

## Teamfights

- **Attack the closest target you can hit safely.** The enemy tank in front is often the right target; reaching for a carry often gets you killed.
- **Stay behind your front line.** Let tanks and fighters take the first hits.
- **Kite.** Attack, move, attack. Use your full range to stay out of the enemy's reach.
- **Keep Flash for escape.** Using Flash to chase rarely helps; using it to escape a diver often saves the fight.
- **Watch for flankers.** Before fights, know where enemy assassins and divers are. If they are missing, play further back.

## What to track

- The enemy support's crowd control cooldown.
- The enemy jungler: bot lane is often their first target.
- Your gold and the cost of your next item.
- Dragon timers and which lane has priority before each dragon.

## Common mistakes

- **Mistake:** Trading when your support is out of position. **Fix:** Trade together or not at all.
- **Mistake:** Farming side lanes alone in the mid game. **Fix:** Farm waves close to your team and vision.
- **Mistake:** Walking forward to hit the enemy carry. **Fix:** Hit the closest safe target and let the fight come to you.
- **Mistake:** Using Flash aggressively. **Fix:** Save Flash for escaping divers unless it wins the game.

## Going deeper

**Farm is your experience too.** Missing a whole wave costs both gold and experience. Prefer a safe recall timing that keeps you in lane for the next wave.

**Learn your auto-attack timing.** Smooth last-hitting and kiting both come from knowing when your attack will land. Practise in practice mode: last-hit, then move, in a steady rhythm.

**Track the enemy's damage.** In fights, know which enemies can kill you quickly. Your positioning should keep you out of their range, even if it means hitting a less valuable target.

## Practice plan

1. In practice mode, last-hit for five minutes with no items, aiming to miss as few minions as you can.
2. In your next three games, before each trade, check the enemy support's main crowd-control ability.
3. After each game, review your deaths: was it a flanker, a skillshot or a lack of vision?
