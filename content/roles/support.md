# Support cheat sheet

- Slug: support
- Role: Support
- Summary: Protect and enable your team, control vision and create plays without needing farm.
- Where you play: The bottom lane early, then wherever vision and fights are needed.
- You win by: Vision control, crowd control and good timing on roams and engages.
- Starter champions: leona, nautilus, soraka, lulu, janna, braum
- Key lessons: MA05, MA19, MI21, MI13, MI11, MI24, MA14, MA41, MA46

## Short answer

The support does not rely on farm. Instead, you help the team through vision, crowd control, healing and shields, and by being in the right place at the right time. In lane, you protect your bot laner and look for chances to start fights. After lane, you move around the map placing wards, helping other lanes and preparing objectives. Supports who climb understand that their impact comes from information and timing: the right ward before an objective, the right engage when the enemy has used their escape, the right peel to keep a carry alive.

## Your jobs

1. **Protect or enable your bot laner.** Help them farm safely and trade when your champion is stronger.
2. **Control vision.** Place wards where your team needs information and remove enemy wards.
3. **Start or stop fights.** Engage supports start fights; enchanters keep their carries alive.
4. **Roam when lane allows it.** Help mid or your jungler when your bot laner is safe.
5. **Communicate.** Share timers, missing enemies and plans with your team.

## Laning phase

- **Buy the support item.** It gives you gold without taking minions from your laner. Follow its tooltip and do not take your carry's farm.
- **Stand where you threaten.** Stay close enough to punish an enemy who steps forward, but not in front of your laner's minions.
- **Trade around cooldowns.** Engage when the enemy's escape or key crowd control is down. Disengage when yours is.
- **Ward against ganks.** A ward in the river or side bush at the right time saves your lane from the enemy jungler.
- **Roam at good moments.** After your lane recalls or when the wave is crashing into the enemy turret, you can roam mid or help your jungler.

## Mid game

- **Vision first.** Before every objective, place wards around it and clear enemy wards with your sweeper.
- **Move with your carries or your jungler.** A support alone in the enemy jungle is a common pick target.
- **Use control wards.** Keep one in your inventory and place it in key bushes or near objectives.
- **Look for picks.** With vision and crowd control, you can catch enemies moving alone. Ping your team before you engage.

## Teamfights

- **Engage supports start fights.** Use your crowd control on the most valuable target you can reach, once your team is close enough to follow.
- **Enchanters peel.** Stay near your carry and use heals, shields and crowd control on whoever dives them.
- **Do not waste key abilities early.** Your main crowd control often decides the fight; use it when it matters most.
- **Stay alive long enough.** A support who dies first leaves the team without vision and peel.

## What to track

- The enemy jungler, especially early: your lane is a frequent gank target.
- Objective timers and the wards needed before each spawn.
- Enemy summoner spells, particularly Flash on carries.
- Your own crowd control and the enemy's key escape abilities.

## Common mistakes

- **Mistake:** Taking your carry's minions. **Fix:** Use the support item's gold sources and let your carry farm.
- **Mistake:** Warding alone deep in enemy territory. **Fix:** Ward with a teammate nearby or in safer areas.
- **Mistake:** Engaging when your team is too far away to follow. **Fix:** Check your allies before starting a fight.
- **Mistake:** Staying bot all game. **Fix:** Roam and ward when your carry is safe and an objective is coming.

## Going deeper

**Vision is a plan, not a habit.** Each ward should answer a question: where is the jungler, is the objective safe, can we push? Place wards for the next play, not only where you are standing.

**Lead with information.** Supports often see the most of the map. Calm, early pings about missing enemies and timers turn your vision into team decisions.

**Know when to stop roaming.** Roaming helps only if your carry stays safe. If the enemy bot lane can dive or freeze on your carry, stay or come back.

## Practice plan

1. In your next three games, place a ward before each objective spawns and note whether it helped.
2. Ping one useful piece of information each minute in the early game.
3. After each game, review one fight: did you use your main crowd control on the most important target?
