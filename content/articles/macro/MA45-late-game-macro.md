# Late-game macro

- Stable lesson ID: MA45
- Slug: late-game-macro
- Category: Macro
- Roles and level: All roles; advanced.
- Summary: Play around long death timers, Baron, Elder and side waves without throwing.
- Prerequisites: Win conditions, Split pushing, Objective setup.
- Observable action: Before leaving your team in the late game, check vision, enemy positions and the next objective.

## Short answer

In the late game, one mistake can end the game. Death timers are long, so losing a fight can cost an inhibitor or the Nexus. Baron and Elder dragon give huge rewards. The team that wins late games keeps the side waves pushed, avoids being caught without vision and takes fights only when they have an advantage. Patience and organisation matter more than mechanics here.

## How it works

Late-game decisions revolve around a few questions.

1. **Where are the side waves?** Pushed side waves force the enemy to answer them, which creates numbers advantages or objective chances.
2. **What does the enemy know?** Moving through unwarded areas alone is the most common late-game throw.
3. **What is the next objective?** Baron, Elder dragon and inhibitors decide games. Set them up early.
4. **What happens if we lose the next fight?** If the answer is that you lose the game, be careful about how you take that fight.

## Hypothetical example

Both teams are full build. Your team has pushed top and bot side waves, and Baron spawns soon. Instead of walking into the enemy jungle to place wards, your team groups mid and sweeps the area around Baron together. The enemy must send someone to catch a side wave, giving your team a numbers advantage. You start the fight while the enemy is spread out, win it and take Baron.

## Going deeper

**The cost of a pick grows.** Being caught alone late in the game can give the enemy a free Baron or inhibitor. Move with a teammate or through warded areas. If you must catch a side wave, do it when you know where enemies are.

**Side lanes and grouping.** A strong duelist pushing one side lane while the team holds the middle can create pressure on two sides at once. This works only if the side laner knows when to back off and the rest of the team avoids fights it cannot win. When the enemy has a big objective available, regroup.

**Baron and Elder.** Baron empowers your minions and helps you push. Elder dragon gives a powerful buff that can decide fights. Treat them as fight starters: set up vision and side waves first, and do not start them when the enemy can collapse on you.

**After a winning fight.** Check how long the enemies are dead. With long death timers, push for inhibitors or the Nexus. With short timers, take a safer objective and reset rather than over-extending.

**Defending when behind.** Clear waves safely, avoid face-checking and wait for the enemy to over-extend. Many late games are lost by the team that tries to force a fight when they are not ready.

## Common mistake and correction

**Mistake:** Walking alone into the enemy jungle to ward or farm just before a major objective spawns.

**Correction:** Group, sweep and ward together, and send the side laner back before the objective window.

## Practice

Review two late-game losses. For each, pause before the decisive fight or pick. Note where the side waves were, which areas had vision and who moved alone. Identify the first decision that made the loss more likely.

## Limits

Objective buffs, death timers and map details change between seasons. Check current tooltips and patch notes. Late-game play depends heavily on team coordination, which varies in solo queue.
